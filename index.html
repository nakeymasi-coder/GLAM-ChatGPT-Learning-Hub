Prompt Anatomy

The 6 layers of a controlled image prompt

01
Subject

Who or what should exist in the image.

02
Features

Appearance, clothing, expression, age, proportions, hair, makeup, object details.

03
Composition

Close-up, full body, centered, poster layout, negative space, camera angle.

04
Rendering

Illustration style, realism, material, texture, commercial or editorial treatment.

05
Lighting & Finish

Studio light, rim light, reflections, gloss, gradients, polished details.

06
Guardrails

Exact text, protected details, exclusions, aspect ratio, what must not change.
